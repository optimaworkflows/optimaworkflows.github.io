What You Need to Know Before You Proceed

What kinds of chargebacks can be disputed?

Shortage claims, OTIF penalties, compliance deductions, and other retailer-specific charges that shipping manifests or proof-of-delivery records can contradict.

Which retailers does this cover?

Major retailers with vendor deduction programs, including Walmart, Target, and Amazon Vendor Central — each handled against that retailer's own deduction codes and dispute windows.

What records are needed?

Retailer remittance/deduction statements, shipping manifests, and proof-of-delivery records for the disputed period.

How are you paid?

40% of the disputable value we identify and document. If no invalid deductions are found, there's no fee, and payment is never based on what the retailer ultimately approves or credits back.

Do you file the dispute with the retailer for us?

Not by default. We identify and document the disputable deductions, formatted and ready for you to file through the retailer's portal. Done-for-you filing is available separately as a monthly retainer add-on.

Can we get on a call to discuss this first?

Everything here runs by email and written form by design — it keeps every finding documented and gets your audit started faster than scheduling a call would. Reply to any email and you'll get a same-business-day written answer.
