Why this exists

The $7,000 CAT Intelligence report is a deployment decision at a point in time. Once crews, materials and capital move into a market, the conditions around that decision keep changing. Weekly Deployment Tracking monitors those changes after deployment so you can see when competitive pressure, permit conditions, labor availability, hazard evidence or other verified zone signals materially shift.
