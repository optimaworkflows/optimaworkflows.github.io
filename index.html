FAQ

The practical questions.

Clear answers before you send anything.

Do I pay anything to start?

No. Each module starts with a free, no-obligation trial using your own historical data.

What if you find nothing?

Then you know. There is no obligation to continue simply because we looked.

What data do you need?

It depends on the audit. We'll tell you exactly which records are relevant before you send anything.

Is my data safe?

Files are used only for the audit or trial you requested and are never sold, shared, or reused. A data-handling agreement is available before you send anything sensitive.

Can one business use more than one service?

Yes. Each module runs independently, so you can have several going at once.

Do you make sales calls?

No sales call is required. Onboarding, findings, and follow-up will happen by email or dedicated user dashboard.

Trial Terms — Plain English

The exact terms of the free trial, no fine print.

  • ✓ A findings snapshot at no cost and no contract

What it doesn't include
