Questions Worth Answering Up Front

Do I have to sign a contract just to see results?

No. You get a free snapshot of what we find, at no cost and no contract. The full, line-by-line findings report — and any further service — is released once you've signed on and paid for that round of findings. That way you always see real, verified findings before anything is ever billed.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are used only for the audit or trial you requested and are never sold, shared, or reused. A data-handling agreement is available before you send anything sensitive.

Can one business use more than one service at the same time?

Yes. Each module runs independently, so you can have several going at once — a vendor audit and a freight audit simultaneously, for example. Most clients start with whichever leak is biggest, then add others whenever they're ready, without finishing one first.
